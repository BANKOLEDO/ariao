Approach — three phases Workshop → Launch

No smoke. No noise.

One workshop to start, weekly builds in the middle, a hard cut at the end. You talk to the people doing the work the whole way through.

Discover

Week 1 — workshop + brief
  • One day together: goals, audience, competitors, and the honest constraints.
  • A sharp one-page brief instead of a 40-slide deck.
  • Positioning and messaging decided in the room, not over three calls.
  • Fixed scope, fixed price, start date on the calendar.

Make

Weeks 2–6 — weekly builds
  • Something clickable every Friday — real pages, real copy, real links.
  • Design and build happen together, so nothing dies in a handoff.
  • Your team gets access from day one; no reveal meetings.
  • Feedback in the tool, gathered once a week, acted on immediately.

Refine

Final weeks — cut and ship
  • We cut until only the necessary remains — copy, motion, all of it.
  • Performance, accessibility, and device passes before launch.
  • Guidelines and a CMS walkthrough so your team can run it.
  • 30 days of post-launch support included, no retainer required.
